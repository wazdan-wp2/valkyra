Wazdan Group

This international organisation operates within the iGaming industry, bringing together companies and teams with diverse areas of expertise, all contributing to the continued development of the Wazdan brand.

Across the Group, specialists create online casino games and solutions for operators in regulated markets worldwide. Their expertise spans areas including technology, product development, design, mathematics, sales, marketing and partner support.

Valkyra forms part of Wazdan Group’s organisational infrastructure, providing support for contractors.

What do we do?

Valkyra provides people-related, administrative and organisational support for Wazdan Group.

Our key areas of activity include:

Coordination of people-related processes

We ensure that the onboarding and ongoing engagement of contractors are handled efficiently and transparently.

Administrative support

We assist with formalities, document management and access to the necessary information.

Organisational support

We provide assistance with day-to-day matters connected with operating and cooperating within Wazdan Group.

Communication

We ensure that important information is communicated clearly, promptly and in an accessible way.

Why join us?

Joining Wazdan Group offers the opportunity to work within an internationalenvironment that brings together a wide range of specialisms, experience and perspectives. We value transparent processes, partnership-based relationships and efficient communication.

Within Wazdan Group, you can expect:

  • cooperation with specialists from a wide range of fields
  • clearly coordinated processes
  • access to ongoing organisational support
  • the opportunity to contribute to the development of an international brand operating within the iGaming industry
